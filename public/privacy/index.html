THE SMALL PRINT

A note on privacy.

This website introduces The Dutchy Finch and helps you find us at Dirty Janes Canberra.

Browsing the website

We do not use advertising pixels or analytics trackers on this website. Fonts, photographs, artwork and the opening animation are served with the site. Our hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver and protect the website.

The little finch

The opening animation starts afresh when you open the website. It does not store a playback preference or any personal information. You can pause, skip or replay the animation at any time. The website also respects your device’s reduced-motion and data-saving preferences where available.

Maps and social media

The embedded Google map loads only after you choose “Explore the map”. Opening the map or following a link to Google Maps, Instagram or Facebook connects you with that service, which has its own privacy practices.

Getting in touch

Enquiries take place by email or through The Dutchy Finch’s social media pages. This website does not collect enquiries or payment information through a form. For a question about your information, please contact The Dutchy Finch on Facebook (opens in a new tab).
