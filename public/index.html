OUR SPECIALITIES

Care for the
next chapter.

A fresh start, a change of space, or a piece with more life to give. It begins with a conversation.

Enquire on Facebook ↗ (opens in a new tab)
01

Estate downsizing

Moving to a smaller home brings decisions about much-loved belongings. Talk to us about downsizing and the pieces you’re ready to part with.

02

Deceased estate clearing

A considered approach to clearing a loved one’s estate, with respect for the belongings and memories involved. Start with a conversation about what you need.

03

Furniture restoration

A beautiful old piece can still have a future. Talk to us about restoring a well-loved find and giving its original character room to shine.

04

Upcycling & restyling

A considered change of finish. A fresh point of view. Reimagine an old favourite so it feels at home in your life today.
